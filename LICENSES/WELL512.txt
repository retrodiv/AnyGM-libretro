WELL512 recurrence attribution

The WELL512 recurrence is adapted from Chris Lomont's public-domain C/C++
listing in Random Number Generation, page 8. The author's public-domain
statement accompanies the listing, and he requests a reference as a courtesy.
Source: https://lomont.org/papers/2008/Lomont_PRNG_2008.pdf

The local adaptation uses fixed-width state owned by the runtime instance.
The state initialization and output scaling are separate from that listing.
The SPDX notice above covers this explanatory text, not the original
public-domain recurrence.
